For the class Mechatronics, our final project was to design, prototype, and test a robot that would participate in a final competition. Our task was to collect as many cubes as possible, when we start at either the yellow or blue side of a square board, with 1" cubes scattered along the middle line, and one opponent robot starting on the other side.

A more detailed final report of the robot and our performance in the final competition is in this google document:
